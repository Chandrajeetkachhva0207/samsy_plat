# Dnyan Narmada Sangam

Build a complete, production-ready, mobile-first website for "मा नर्मदा आध्यात्मिक शिक्षण संस्था" (Maa Narmada Adhyatmik Shikshan Sanstha), a spiritual and educational trust in Alandi Devachi, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

Gurukul initiative: "मा नर्मदा आध्यात्मिक गुरुकुल, आळंदी देवाची"

Tagline: "शिक्षण • संस्कार • अध्यात्म • वारकरी परंपरा"

Founder President: ह.भ.प. संतोष महाराज पवार

Address: ता. खेड, जि. पुणे, महाराष्ट्र

The institution has been running the gurukul for 12 years in Alandi, the sacred land of Sant Dnyaneshwar Maharaj.

## LANGUAGE

- Primary language: Marathi (Devanagari). Add an English toggle (मराठी | English) in the header.

- Fonts: "Mukta" or "Noto Sans Devanagari" for body text, "Tiro Devanagari Marathi" or "Yatra One" for headings. Large, clear, readable text.

- All content must be stored so it can be edited in both languages.

## DESIGN

- Simple, spiritual, dignified, clean. NO loud or flashy colours.

- Palette: saffron/kesari (#E8891D or similar, used sparingly), white, soft earthy/sandalwood tones (#F6EFE4, #8B6B4A), deep maroon or brown for text accents.

- Generous white space, short text blocks, card-based layout, soft shadows, rounded corners.

- Subtle decorative elements only (a thin saffron border, a small pataka/flag motif, a tulsi-mala divider). No heavy animations. Only gentle fade-in on scroll.

- Fully responsive: mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop. Fast loading, lazy-loaded and compressed images, good Lighthouse score.

- Accessible: proper contrast, alt text on every image, keyboard navigation.

## SACRED IMAGERY (IMPORTANT)

Use respectful images of these three in the Hero, About section and a "संतपरंपरा / Our Inspiration" strip:

1. श्री विठ्ठल-रखुमाई (Shri Vitthal–Rakhumai, Pandharpur)

2. संत ज्ञानेश्वर महाराज (Sant Dnyaneshwar Maharaj, Alandi)

3. संत तुकाराम महाराज (Sant Tukaram Maharaj, Dehu)

- Create image slots with the file names: /images/vitthal-rakhumai.jpg, /images/sant-dnyaneshwar.jpg, /images/sant-tukaram.jpg. I will supply the actual images. Use tasteful placeholders until then.

- Display them respectfully: never cropped awkwardly, never distorted, never placed in the footer or in a decorative or disrespectful position. Place them at the top or in a dedicated dignified section, with the name captioned beneath.

- Add a line: "राम कृष्ण हरी" / "ज्ञानोबा-तुकाराम" as a devotional greeting in the hero.

- Do not alter faces or apply AI filters to any real photo (founder, students, events). Use originals as provided.

## NAVIGATION

Sticky header with logo + name. Menu:

मुख्यपृष्ठ | आमच्याविषयी | संस्थापक अध्यक्ष | गुरुकुल | उपक्रम | विद्यार्थी व सुविधा | कार्यक्रम | फोटो गॅलरी | व्हिडिओ | कार्य अहवाल | सहकार्य करा | संपर्क

- Mobile: clean hamburger drawer.

- A separate prominent saffron button "सहकार्य करा / देणगी" always visible in the header.

- Floating WhatsApp/call button on mobile (use placeholder number).

## PAGES AND SECTIONS

### 1. Home (मुख्यपृष्ठ)

- Hero: high-quality gurukul/activity photo (slider or single), institution name, tagline, short intro. Include the Vitthal-Rakhumai, Dnyaneshwar and Tukaram images in a dignified way.

- 4 CTA buttons: "आमच्याविषयी", "गुरुकुलाचे उपक्रम", "सहकार्य करा", "संपर्क करा".

- Short introduction: "आळंदी देवाची या संत ज्ञानेश्वर महाराजांच्या पावन भूमीत गेल्या १२ वर्षांपासून गुरुकुलाच्या माध्यमातून विद्यार्थ्यांना शालेय शिक्षणासोबत आध्यात्मिक संस्कार, वारकरी परंपरा, संतविचार, कीर्तन, प्रवचन आणि भारतीय संस्कृतीची ओळख करून देण्याचा प्रयत्न केला जात आहे."

- Key activities as icon cards: शालेय शिक्षण, आध्यात्मिक शिक्षण, वारकरी संस्कार, बाल संस्कार शिबिर, कीर्तन व प्रवचन, सांस्कृतिक कार्यक्रम, सामाजिक उपक्रम.

- Founder President card: photo, "ह.भ.प. संतोष महाराज पवार", "संस्थापक अध्यक्ष, मा नर्मदा आध्यात्मिक शिक्षण संस्था", link to full page.

- "१२ वर्षांचा प्रवास": timeline with editable milestones and photos.

- Upcoming events (auto-pulled, next 3).

- Photo gallery preview and latest video.

- Support appeal: "विद्यार्थ्यांच्या शिक्षण, संस्कार आणि गुरुकुलाच्या विकासासाठी समाजातील हितचिंतकांचे सहकार्य आवश्यक आहे." + button.

### 2. आमच्याविषयी

Establishment, objectives, 12-year journey, work in Alandi, efforts for students, combination of spiritual and school education, future goals. Use ONLY editable placeholder text where facts are not provided. Do NOT invent statistics, numbers, awards or claims. Add an admin-editable "Verified information" field.

### 3. संस्थापक अध्यक्ष

Official photo (unaltered), introduction, role in the institution, brief work summary. Content editable.

### 4. गुरुकुल

Identity of the gurukul, educational environment, spiritual environment, daily routine (editable timetable card: प्रार्थना, नामस्मरण, अभ्यास, संस्कार वर्ग, कीर्तन-सराव etc. as placeholders), facilities, working method. Only list actually available facilities. Make them editable.

### 5. उपक्रम (Activities)

Separate cards, each with an icon, image and short description, opening a detail view:

शालेय शिक्षण, अभ्यास मार्गदर्शन, प्रार्थना, नामस्मरण, संतविचार, वारकरी परंपरा, कीर्तन, प्रवचन, बाल संस्कार शिबिरे, सांस्कृतिक कार्यक्रम, सामाजिक उपक्रम.

### 6. विद्यार्थी व सुविधा

Cards: निवास, भोजन, अभ्यासिका, संस्कार वर्ग, सांस्कृतिक उपक्रम, क्रीडा, ग्रंथालय/अभ्यास साहित्य, स्वच्छता, इतर सुविधा.

PRIVACY: never display student Aadhaar numbers, home addresses, personal phone numbers, dates of birth or other sensitive data. Student photos/names only when the admin ticks a "Consent obtained" checkbox on the upload.

### 7. कार्यक्रम (Events)

- Two tabs: "आगामी कार्यक्रम" (upcoming) and "मागील कार्यक्रम" (past). Events auto-move to past after the date passes.

- Fields: name, date, weekday (auto), time, venue, kirtankar/speakers/chief guests, description, photos.

- Event detail page, share button (WhatsApp), and "Add to calendar".

- Admin can add/edit/delete events without a developer.

### 8. फोटो गॅलरी

- Categories with filter: गुरुकुल, विद्यार्थी, कीर्तन, वारकरी कार्यक्रम, बाल संस्कार शिबिर, धार्मिक कार्यक्रम, सांस्कृतिक कार्यक्रम, सामाजिक उपक्रम, विशेष कार्यक्रम.

- Lightbox with swipe, captions, lazy loading, optional bulk upload and auto-compression in admin. Photos must display in full clarity.

### 9. व्हिडिओ

- Admin adds YouTube/other official video links. Each has title, date, short description and auto-embedded thumbnail. Responsive grid, lazy-loaded embeds.

### 10. कार्य अहवाल (Annual Reports)

- Year-wise list (e.g., २०२५–२६, २०२६–२७) with view online and PDF download. Each report can cover activities, students, educational work, spiritual programs, social work, photos and future plans. Admin uploads PDF per year.

### 11. भविष्यातील योजना (Future Plans)

Section or page listing: own land for the gurukul, student accommodation, dining hall, classrooms, study room, prayer/meditation hall, kirtan-cultural hall, playground, drinking water, toilets, kitchen, library and other facilities. Label everything clearly as "प्रस्तावित / भविष्यातील योजना". Never show incomplete plans as completed. Optional progress-status tags: प्रस्तावित / सुरू / पूर्ण.

### 12. सहकार्य करा (Support / Donate)

- Types of support (editable cards): आर्थिक सहकार्य, अन्नदान/भोजन, शैक्षणिक साहित्य, वस्तुरूप मदत, वेळ/सेवा.

- Show ONLY the institution's official bank account / UPI / QR (placeholders: [Bank Name], [Account Name], [A/C No.], [IFSC], [UPI ID], [QR image]). Never a personal account.

- Donation receipt info, contact for donations, and tax exemption/80G details ONLY if valid (admin toggle to show/hide).

- Optional "I have donated" form (name, amount, transaction ref, contact) that saves to admin for receipt follow-up.

- A note: "देणगीपूर्वी कृपया अधिकृत माहितीची खात्री करा."

### 13. संस्था नोंदणी / प्रमाणपत्रे

Show only the certificate name, number and validity (e.g., Trust registration, 12A, 80G, PAN) as text placeholders. Do not publish full documents containing signatures, bank details or ID numbers unless the admin approves.

### 14. संपर्क (Contact)

- Address: मा नर्मदा आध्यात्मिक गुरुकुल, आळंदी देवाची, ता. खेड, जि. पुणे, महाराष्ट्र

- Phone: [अधिकृत नंबर], Email: [अधिकृत ई-मेल]

- Embedded Google Map: [अधिकृत Google Maps लोकेशन]

- Contact form: नाव, मोबाईल, ई-मेल, विषय, संदेश → saved in admin dashboard AND emailed to the institution's official email. Add spam protection (reCAPTCHA/honeypot), validation and a success message.

- Click-to-call, WhatsApp link, social links (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram — placeholders).

### 15. Footer

Institution name, gurukul name, quick links, contact, social links, "© [वर्ष] मा नर्मदा आध्यात्मिक शिक्षण संस्था. सर्व हक्क राखीव." Privacy policy link.

## ADMIN PANEL (CMS) — CRITICAL

Secure login (email + password, optional 2FA, role-based: Super Admin / Editor). The institution's staff must be able to update WITHOUT a developer:

- Page text (Marathi and English)

- Events (add/edit/delete, upcoming/past)

- Photo gallery (categories, bulk upload, delete)

- Video links

- Annual report PDFs

- Founder details, timeline, activities, facilities, future plans

- Donation/bank details and QR

- Contact info, social links

- Contact form submissions (view, export CSV, mark as read)

- Site-wide settings and SEO fields

Include image-upload compression, draft/publish toggle, and a simple, mostly-Marathi admin UI.

## SEO, ANALYTICS AND DISCOVERY

- Meta titles/descriptions in Marathi + English, Open Graph images for WhatsApp sharing, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, clean URLs.

- Schema.org markup: Organization / EducationalOrganization / Event, with correct name, address and phone.

- Google Analytics/Search Console hooks and Google Business Profile link (placeholders).

- Favicon and logo slots. 404 page.

## SECURITY

HTTPS enforced, input validation and sanitisation, rate-limited forms, secure headers, hashed passwords, backups (daily), no sensitive student data stored publicly, cookie/privacy notice.

## TECH SUGGESTION

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS + Supabase/Firebase (auth, database, storage) OR a headless CMS (Sanity/Strapi). Deploy on Vercel/Netlify. Domain, hosting, database and admin accounts must be under the institution's own official email and ownership, and the site must be fully transferable to another developer.

## CONTENT RULES

- Use placeholders like [अधिकृत नंबर] and [अधिकृत ई-मेल] wherever real data is missing. Never invent phone numbers, registration numbers, bank details, statistics or claims.

- Keep the tone respectful, devotional and humble. No exaggeration.

- Seed the site with sample Marathi content based on the details above, clearly marked as editable.
